# AI Automation Policy — initial

## Allowed automatically

- create event/audit records;
- create AI jobs;
- prepare summaries and drafts;
- detect missing fields or operational anomalies;
- generate internal recommendations for review.

## Human approval required

- cancel an order;
- refund or capture money;
- change price;
- publish BB610 VERIFIED status;
- send non-template advice that can materially affect product use;
- change ad budget/campaign state;
- create or change supplier purchase orders.

## Forbidden to AI as source of truth

- payment status;
- inventory quantity;
- final order total;
- carrier delivery status;
- manufacturer technical data not supported by approved sources.
